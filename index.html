About

I am an MSc Physics student at ETH Zürich specializing in astrophysics and exoplanet science. My current research focuses on the LIFE space mission, nulling interferometry, and exoplanet mission-yield simulations.

For my Master's thesis, I am developing simulation methods to connect LIFE instrument performance with mission-level observing strategies and detection probabilities. My background in mechanical engineering shapes my interest in the link between astrophysical science requirements, instrumentation, and mission design.

Research

Master's thesis · ETH Zürich

Guaranteed Catch — Designing LIFE Around At-Least-One Detection

Studying whether optimizing the probability of at least one successful exoplanet detection leads to different target selection and integration-time allocation than optimizing conventional expected detection yield.

LIFE · Nulling interferometry

Interferometer simulation

Developing a static Fourier-transform-based model to build a detailed understanding of nulling-interferometer response, signal formation, and fundamental noise treatment.

Mission simulation

EXOSIMS integration

Building toward a reusable LIFE-capable implementation in EXOSIMS for population-scale Monte Carlo simulations and observing-strategy studies.
