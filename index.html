About

I am an MSc Physics student at ETH Zürich specializing in astrophysics and exoplanet science. My current research focuses on the LIFE space mission, nulling interferometry, and exoplanet mission-yield simulations.

For my Master's thesis, I am developing simulation methods that connect LIFE instrument performance with mission-level observing strategies and detection probabilities. My mechanical-engineering background informs my interest in the connection between science requirements, instrumentation, and mission design.

Research

Master's thesis · ETH Zürich

Guaranteed Catch — Designing LIFE Around At-Least-One Detection

Investigating whether optimizing the probability of at least one successful exoplanet detection produces different target selection and integration-time allocation than optimizing expected detection yield.

LIFE · Nulling interferometry

Interferometer simulation

Developing a static Fourier-transform-based simulation to study interferometer response, signal formation, and fundamental noise treatment.

Mission simulation

EXOSIMS integration

Building toward a reusable LIFE-capable EXOSIMS implementation for population-scale Monte Carlo simulations and observing-strategy studies.
